Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how AFF Inc. handles information in its five independent quick-recipe applications. The applications are designed to provide ready-made recipes, search, favorites, cooking progress, notes, and shopping lists.

Effective date: August 24, 2026

Important: These applications do not require an account or login. Recipe content and user-created favorites, notes, cooking progress, and shopping-list data are stored locally on the device. The applications use Google Mobile Ads to display advertisements, so Google may process certain device and usage information as described below.

Information stored locally on your device

The applications work offline for their core recipe features. Depending on how you use an application, it may store the following information locally on your device using Android local storage:

  • Favorite recipe selections.
  • Cooking-step progress and the time of the latest cooking activity, so the application can offer a temporary “continue cooking” option.
  • Checked ingredients and shopping-list items.
  • Recipe notes that you choose to write.
  • Recently opened recipe identifiers and display preferences such as dark mode.

AFF Inc. does not receive this locally stored recipe activity through a company server. You can remove it by using the relevant in-app controls, clearing the application’s storage in Android settings, or uninstalling the application. Because there is no user account, there is no account profile or account deletion process to request.

Advertising and Google Mobile Ads

The applications use the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display banner, interstitial, app-open, and native advertisements. Google states that the Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share an IP address, user product interactions, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers for advertising, analytics, and fraud-prevention purposes. This information is handled by Google and its partners under their own policies and controls.

For more information, please review Google’s Privacy Policy and Google’s Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure. Android advertising-ID controls and other privacy choices may also be available in your device settings. AFF Inc. does not use Firebase Analytics in these applications.

Information we do not request

The applications do not require registration and do not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, location, files, or payment information. The core recipe features do not require an internet connection. Internet access is used by the advertising SDK when an advertisement is available.

Sharing and disclosure

AFF Inc. does not sell the locally stored recipe data described above. When you deliberately use Android’s share feature, the text you choose to share is sent to the application or service you select in the Android share sheet. Google Mobile Ads may process information for advertising and related operational purposes as described in the advertising section of this policy.

Data security

Local recipe data remains within the application’s Android storage area and is protected by the device and operating system security model. Advertising-related network traffic is handled by the Google Mobile Ads SDK and its infrastructure. No security method can guarantee absolute security, so you should use the security controls available on your device.

Children’s privacy

These applications are general-audience recipe applications and are not directed specifically to children. They do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to AFF Inc., please contact us so that we can review the concern.

Data retention and deletion

Locally stored recipe preferences and notes remain on your device until you delete them in the application, clear the application’s storage, or uninstall the application. AFF Inc. does not maintain an account database for these applications. Data handled by Google Mobile Ads is subject to Google’s retention and deletion practices, which are described in Google’s privacy resources linked above.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when application features, advertising services, or legal requirements change. The revised version will be published at this URL with a new effective date. You should review this page periodically.
