Privacy Policy

Application: Quick Juices and Drinks
Package ID: com.asaersreeah.ramdan
Effective date: August 24, 2026

Important: This is the privacy policy for Quick Juices and Drinks only. It is a general-audience recipe application. It does not require an account or login.

What the application does

Quick Juices and Drinks provides 200 ready-made juice and drink recipes, recipe search, favorites, preparation progress, personal recipe notes, serving adjustment, and shopping-list features. Core recipe features are designed to work offline.

Information stored locally

Depending on how you use this application, Android local storage may contain your favorite recipes, checked ingredients, shopping-list items, preparation-step progress, recent recipe identifiers, display preferences, and notes that you choose to write. This information stays in the application’s local storage on your device and is not sent to an AFF Inc. account server.

You can delete local data through the in-app controls, Android application-storage settings, or by uninstalling the application. There is no user account and therefore no account profile to delete.

Google Mobile Ads

This application uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display banner, interstitial, app-open, and native advertisements. Google states that the SDK may automatically collect and share an IP address, user product interactions, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Google and its partners process that information under their own policies.

Read Google’s Privacy Policy and Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure. Android advertising-ID controls and other privacy choices may be available in device settings. AFF Inc. does not use Firebase Analytics in this application.

Information we do not request

Quick Juices and Drinks does not require registration and does not request contacts, camera, microphone, location, files, or payment information. Internet access is used by the advertising SDK when an advertisement is available.

Sharing

AFF Inc. does not sell the locally stored recipe data described above. If you use the share feature, the text you deliberately choose to share is passed to the application or service you select in Android’s share sheet.

Security, retention, and deletion

Local data is protected by the Android operating system and remains until you remove it or uninstall the application. Advertising-related data is subject to Google’s policies and retention practices. No security method can guarantee absolute security.

Children’s privacy

This application is not directed specifically to children and does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Contact us if you have a related concern.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the application, advertising services, or applicable requirements change. The updated version will be published at this URL with a new effective date.
